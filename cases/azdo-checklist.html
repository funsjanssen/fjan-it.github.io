Azure DevOps Checklist Extension

Transform your Azure DevOps workflow with powerful checklist capabilities. FJAN IT has developed an innovative extension that helps teams follow consistent processes and ensures nothing falls through the cracks. Create reusable checklist templates, assign them to work item types, and ensure consistent processes across your team.

This extension is perfect for teams following structured processes like Definition of Done, code review checklists, deployment procedures, or testing protocols. With seamless integration into Azure DevOps work items, teams can improve their workflow and ensure all important steps are followed.

Key Features
  • 🎯 Template Management - Create and manage reusable checklist templates with drag-and-drop organization
  • 📋 Work Item Integration - Seamlessly embed checklists directly into work item forms
  • 🔄 Progress Tracking - Visual progress indicators show completion status at a glance
  • ⚙️ Field Mapping - Store checklist data in custom work item fields as markdown
  • 🎨 Multi-Template Support - Apply multiple checklist templates to the same work item type
  • 💾 Smart Persistence - Changes are saved only when you modify checklist items
  • 📈 Change History - Track checklist modifications through Azure DevOps field history and audit trails
Perfect for Structured Processes

The extension is ideal for teams that want to ensure consistency and quality in their development processes. Whether it's Definition of Done criteria, code review checklists, deployment procedures, or testing protocols - this extension ensures your team stays aligned and doesn't skip important steps.

With visual progress indicators and smart persistence functionality, teams can organize their work more efficiently while managers get real-time insights into the progress of various processes across the entire team.
